    Practical actions to reduce your impact

    Examples of the kind of suggestions you will see. These are fixed samples, not AI-generated.

    💡 Reduce short car trips

    Potential impact: Medium

    Replace 2 short car journeys per week with walking, cycling, or public transport.

    🔌 Cut unnecessary electricity use

    Potential impact: Medium

    Switch off standby devices and use efficient lighting.

    ♻️ Improve recycling

    Potential impact: Low

    Separate paper, plastic and organic waste at home.

    🥗 Reduce food waste

    Potential impact: Medium

    Plan meals for the week and use leftovers.

    🚆 Choose lower-impact transport

    Potential impact: High

    Pick trains or buses over flights and solo car trips where you can.
